Methodology

Research workflow

From a research question to reproducible computational evidence.

Research checklist

  1. Define a research question, scope, language and population of interest.
  2. Document source selection, rights, permissions and retrieval date.
  3. Keep raw data separate from cleaned and derived data; record transformations.
  4. Specify tokenization, normalization, filtering and analytical parameters.
  5. Evaluate the method and inspect counterexamples, not only aggregate results.
  6. Interpret results in context; distinguish association from causal claims.
  7. Preserve code, environment information, provenance and permitted outputs.

Source labels

Synthetic teaching data
Invented examples; not empirical evidence.
Historical data
Fixed records from an earlier context; not necessarily current or representative.
Live retrieved data
Record retrieval time, endpoint, sample selection and possible changes.
Student-created data
Document collection, privacy, consent where applicable and limitations.

Ethics and privacy

Respect source policies, applicable law, copyright and personal data requirements. Avoid storing credentials or sensitive texts in public repositories. Prefer small, documented samples and proportionate acquisition. Do not publish assessed solutions or private student work.
